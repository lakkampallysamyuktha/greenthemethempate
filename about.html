Our History

From a Garage in California
to 120 Countries

What started as a small team of environmental engineers with a big idea has grown into one of the world's most trusted names in renewable energy.

2009

Company Founded

Started in California with a focus on residential solar installations. First 50 homes powered by clean energy.

2012

Commercial Breakthrough

Secured first utility-scale solar project — a 40MW installation powering 12,000 homes in Nevada.

2014

Expansion into Wind Energy

Acquired AeroWind Tech and launched our commercial wind turbine division across the Midwest.

2019

Global Headquarters

Opened European and Asian hubs in Amsterdam and Singapore. Operating in 100+ countries.

2024

1 Million Installations

A landmark moment — our one millionth renewable energy system installed, displacing 2.5M tons of CO₂ annually.
