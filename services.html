FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does a solar installation take?

A typical residential installation takes 1‑3 days once permits are approved. The entire process from consultation to turning the system on usually takes 4‑8 weeks depending on your local utility and government permit processing times.

Do I still need to be connected to the grid?

Most of our customers choose a grid‑tied system, allowing them to sell excess power back to the utility company (net metering). However, with our battery storage solutions, complete off‑grid systems are entirely possible.

What happens during a power outage?

Standard grid‑tied solar systems will automatically shut off during an outage for safety reasons. If you have our battery storage system installed, your home will automatically switch to backup power in milliseconds, keeping your lights on.

Are there financing options available?

Yes! We offer zero‑down solar loans, leases, and Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs). We also help you navigate and apply for federal and state tax incentives to ensure you get the best financial return on your investment.
